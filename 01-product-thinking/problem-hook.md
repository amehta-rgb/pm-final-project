## 1. Chosen scenario

**Path:** StreamLine Spotlight

## 2. The strategic crisis

The company doesn’t collapse in 12 months. It loses the option to fix this cheaply. By month 12, it is likely to have weaker brand perception, a shrinking quality-seeking base, higher costs per retained subscriber, and fewer strategic choices than it has today.

## 3. The moment of misery

It’s Friday night. A quality-seeking viewer opens StreamLine, scrolls a wall of 15,000 titles for four minutes, finds nothing that signals “this is worth my evening,” and leaves. They don’t complain. They open a different tab.

## 4. Problem hook

Quality-seeking viewers are already doing StreamLine’s curation job themselves, off-platform, and every month we let them keep doing it, they get closer to deciding we’re not worth paying for, taking our most loyal, most vocal subscribers and our last chance at a cheap pivot with them.

## 5. Value proposition

Spotlight tells you what’s worth your evening, so you never have to leave the app to decide.
